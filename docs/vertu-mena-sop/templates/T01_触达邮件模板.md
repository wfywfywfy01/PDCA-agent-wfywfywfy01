# T01 触达邮件 / 私信模板

## 英文邮件主题

`VERTU Official Distribution — IWC-Model Boutique Partnership for [Country]`

## 英文正文（可复制）

```
Hi [Name],

I'm [Your Name] from VERTU, responsible for official distribution in [Country].

We are appointing a local partner to operate an IWC-style brand boutique
(80㎡+ experience store: trial, customization, VIP lounge), positioned
alongside fine watch and jewelry retail — not a mass electronics counter.

I'd value 15 minutes on WhatsApp to share the partnership model,
store location logic, and sell-in framework.

Are you available this week?

Best regards,
[Name]
[Title] | VERTU
[WhatsApp] | [Email]
```

## WhatsApp 短讯

```
Hi [Name], this is [Name] from VERTU.
Sent you an email on official boutique distribution in [Country].
15-min chat this week? Happy to share on WhatsApp.
```

## 波斯语方向（Sana 细化）

要点必须包含：

1. 官方 VERTU 独代 / 授权机会  
2. 对标 IWC 精品店模型（非柜台）  
3. 邀请 15 分钟 WhatsApp  
4. 不承诺违规「全球联保」话术  

## 阿拉伯语

由海湾阿语 BD 本地化；结构同英文：官方身份 + 精品店模型 + 约短会。
